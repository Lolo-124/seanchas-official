The Birch Girl

Aghast, Eilidh let the knife fall.

It clattered off a root and went sliding out of reach. The branches closed around her, and she fell to her knees.

“I’m sorry. I’m so sorry, I didn’t think, I just. I needed to help my Gran and I panicked. Please.”

She sounded frantic even to her own ears.

The girl did not speak for a time. Her expression was austere.

“You’ve shed blood. By right you owe me in return. Blood for blood, or time for blood. Choose.” Thin slivers of evening light came through the branches. “The sun is going, so you must be quick.”
