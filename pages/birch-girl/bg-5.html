The Birch Girl

“Aye,” Eilidh said,

“Please. Thank you.” She bit her tongue straight after. You were never supposed to thank the Folk. The girl continued to pull sprigs from her head one by one, wincing slightly each time. She pressed the bundle into Eilidh’s hands. They weighed next to nothing.

“A handful of these will light your fire and keep the folk from your door till dawn if you tend it well. An armful would keep the whole glen clear.”

Her peat-dark eyes were steady. “It’s your decision how much.”

Eilidh looked at the thinning leaves around the birch girl’s head and thought of Gran’s ailing health. Wouldn’t it be better to play it safe?
