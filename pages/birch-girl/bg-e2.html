The Birch Girl

Gran’s bed was empty the next morning when Eilidh finally stumbled home.

Her windows were open and her baffies were missing.

Mum called the police to report a missing person, Dad searched the town, and Bess whined whenever they passed the birch on their walks. Eilidh spent her spare time combing the woods. Nothing was ever found of Gran.

Every Beltane after Gran left, Eilidh would walk back up to the ditch and wait, salt in her pockets, and near midnight the host ribboned its way up the path. Gran rode through it, younger, straight-backed, and laughing. She never looked at Eilidh.

Eilidh told herself that Gran was happy. She didn’t know if it was true or not.
